About

What I do

I teach educational technology and the pedagogy of physical science, supervise doctoral and M.Ed research, and help plan B.Ed and M.Ed curricula for the University of Kerala, including the integrated B.Sc. B.Ed. and B.Ed. M.Ed. programmes.

Signature project

A UGC-funded Major Research Project (2012–2014, ₹7,26,100) led to an e-portfolio platform where student teachers upload work, assessed by rubrics, and employers can view it. Described as the first of its kind in Indian teacher education.

teacherseportfolio.com
